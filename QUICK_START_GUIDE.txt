WHAT THE BACKTEST DOES
================================================================================

1. Tests BOTH algorithms:
   - V5 (Volume-Focused Scorer)
   - Momentum (Price-Action Focused Scorer)

2. For EACH trade:
   - Determines grade (A+/A/A-/B+/B/B-) from scorer
   - Splits position into 3 tranches based on grade:
     * A+/A: 25% T1, 35% T2, 40% T3 (big runner)
     * A-/B+: 33% T1, 33% T2, 34% T3 (balanced)
     * B/B-: 50% T1, 50% T2, 0% T3 (no runner)

3. Tracks exits:
   - T1 @ +50% option gain
   - T2 @ +100% option gain
   - T3 @ +150% option gain (or trailing stop -25%)
   - Stop @ -50% option loss
   - Time stop @ 36 hours

4. Generates statistics:
   - Win rate
   - Total return
   - Tranche hit rates (what % hit T1, T2, T3)
   - Average hold time
   - Max drawdown

TROUBLESHOOTING
