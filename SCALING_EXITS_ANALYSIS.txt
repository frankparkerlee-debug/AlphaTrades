NEW STRATEGY: GRADE-BASED SCALING EXITS
================================================================================

Position Sizing by Grade:
┌────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Grade  │ T1        T2        T3        Strategy            │
├────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A+/A   │ 25%  @    35%  @    40%  @    Let winners run!    │
│        │ +50%      +100%     +150%     (big runner)         │
├────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A-/B+  │ 33%  @    33%  @    34%  @    Balanced approach   │
│        │ +50%      +100%     +150%                          │
├────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ B/B-   │ 50%  @    50%  @     0%       Take profits fast   │
│        │ +50%      +100%     (none)    (no runner)          │
└────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Exit Targets:
- T1: +50% option gain (lock in partial profit)
- T2: +100% option gain (secure more profit)
- T3: +150% option gain OR trailing stop -25% from peak

Risk Management:
- Stop Loss: -50% (applies to FULL position, tighter than +50% target)
- Max Hold: 36 hours (vs. 72 hours - reduces theta decay risk)
- T3 Trailing Stop: -25% from peak (protects runners)

KEY IMPROVEMENTS:
✅ Partial profit taking reduces risk while maintaining upside
✅ A+ setups get bigger runners (40% held for T3)
✅ Weak setups exit faster (no T3 runner)
✅ Shorter hold time reduces theta decay
✅ Trailing stop protects T3 runner profits

WHY SCALING EXITS WORK FOR OPTIONS
================================================================================

1. OPTIONS THETA DECAY:
   - Options lose 1-2% value per day (time decay)
   - 36-hour max hold vs. 72 hours = 50% less decay
   - Estimated theta savings: +2-3% per trade

2. VOLATILITY MEAN REVERSION:
   - Big moves often reverse quickly
   - Partial profit-taking captures profit before reversal
   - Full position would give back gains

3. PSYCHOLOGICAL BENEFIT:
   - "House money effect" - T3 runner uses T1/T2 profits
   - Reduced stress from partial profits
   - Less FOMO from early exits

4. CAPITAL EFFICIENCY:
   - Faster position closes = capital recycles faster
   - More trades per year with same capital
   - Compounding effect

5. GRADE-BASED SIZING:
   - A+ setups deserve bigger runners (statistically better)
   - B setups deserve fast exits (statistically weaker)
   - Risk-proportional allocation
