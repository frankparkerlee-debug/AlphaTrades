STRATEGY OVERVIEW
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
Original strategy barely profitable (49% win rate, +6% returns over 2 years)
- Fixed exits (+50% target, -30% stop)
- No differentiation between A+ and B- setups
- 72-hour hold = excessive theta decay

SOLUTION:
Grade-based scaling exits with 3 tranches per position

┌────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Grade  │ Tranche Allocation                                      │
├────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A+/A   │ 25% @ +50% | 35% @ +100% | 40% @ +150% (big runner)   │
│ A-/B+  │ 33% @ +50% | 33% @ +100% | 34% @ +150% (balanced)     │
│ B/B-   │ 50% @ +50% | 50% @ +100% | 0% @ +150% (fast exit)     │
└────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

IMPROVEMENTS:
✅ Partial profit-taking increases win rate
✅ A+ setups get bigger runners (deserve it statistically)
✅ B- setups exit faster (lower quality)
✅ 36-hour hold reduces theta decay by 50%
✅ Tighter stop (-50%) cuts losses faster
✅ Trailing stop protects T3 runner profits

================================================================================
WHY THIS WORKS
================================================================================

1. PARTIAL PROFIT-TAKING
   Many trades reverse after hitting +50%. Locking in T1 profit before
   reversal increases win rate significantly.

2. GRADE-BASED SIZING
   A+ setups (90+ score) statistically outperform B- setups (60 score).
   They deserve bigger runners (40% vs. 0%).

3. THETA DECAY REDUCTION
   Options lose 1-2% value per day (time decay). 36-hour hold vs. 72-hour
   hold cuts decay in half = +2-3% better returns per trade.

4. TIGHTER STOP
   -50% stop (vs. -30%) exits losers faster, limiting damage.

5. TRAILING STOP
   T3 tranche uses trailing stop (-25% from peak) to protect runner
   profits while letting winners run.
